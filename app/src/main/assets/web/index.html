Speak it.
Wispr writes it.

Talk in Hindi, English, Hinglish or 23 more languages. Get clean text you can paste into WhatsApp, Gmail, notes, anywhere.

  1. Get a free Groq key

    Open console.groq.com/keys, sign in, tap Create API Key and copy it. It starts with gsk_.

  2. Paste it here

  3. Allow permissions and talk

    Allow the mic and the Wispr bubble once. Then open any keyboard in any app, tap the bubble, speak, and your words get typed right there.

Your key is locked with Android Keystore encryption on this phone and never shown again. Audio goes straight from your phone to Groq over HTTPS. No Wispr server, no account, no tracking.

Finish setup

Allow these once. Wispr then works over every keyboard, in every app.

    Android says "Restricted setting"? Tap , then ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings. Then come back and turn on the Wispr bubble.
