## Wispr by Deepu Gupta

Speak in Hindi, English, Hinglish or 23 more languages. Wispr types it for you, in any app.

**What's inside**
- Wispr bubble pops up with any keyboard (Gboard, SwiftKey, Samsung, anything). Tap to talk, tap again and your words get typed straight into the box. Or hold to talk, release to type.
- Text is also copied, and saved in the app's Recent list.
- Auto-retry: if the internet drops, Wispr retries on its own. If it still fails, your audio is saved; tap Retry on the bubble, the notification, or in the app.
- Write, Polish and Translate modes, Roman-letters option, custom spellings.
- Dark mode toggle.
- Security: your Groq key and history are encrypted with Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM). HTTPS only, no backups, no tracking, no Wispr server.

**Install**
1. Download the `.apk` below and open it. Allow "Install unknown apps" if asked.
2. Open Wispr, paste your free Groq key (console.groq.com/keys), tap Verify.
3. Tap **Allow** on each permission. For the bubble, if Android says *Restricted setting*: App info → ⋮ → **Allow restricted settings**, then turn on **Wispr bubble** in Accessibility.
